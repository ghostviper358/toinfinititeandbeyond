# Universal Helmet Requirements

## Overview
The final and **most powerful** piece of the **Universal armor set**, completing it after the
chestplate, leggings and boots (see the other `universal_*.md` docs). Its powers are a step above
the rest of the set: a laser, stopping time, and controlling minds.

## Defensive Stats
- **Armor: 999**, **Toughness: 6**, **40% knockback resistance** - same as the rest of the set.
- **+100 max health (50 extra hearts)** while worn - the most of any piece. With the full set
  that's 110 extra hearts.
- **Durability: unbreakable**.

## Abilities

### Cosmic Eye Beam
- Press the **Eye Beam key (R by default)** to fire a purple laser from your eyes, up to **64
  blocks**.
- Blasts through every target in a straight line: **500 damage** each, and sets them on fire.
- Stops at solid blocks (doesn't break them).
- **1.5 second cooldown.**

### Time Stop
- Press the **Time Stop key (G by default)** to freeze time for **5 seconds** within **32 blocks**.
- Every mob, other player, arrow and other projectile is held perfectly still in mid-air. You keep
  moving and fighting freely.
- When time starts again, frozen arrows and projectiles carry on flying.
- **15 second cooldown** after time starts again.

### Mind Control
- Hostile mobs within **16 blocks** can't target you - they turn on the nearest other hostile mob
  instead, with a purple swirl over their heads.

## Who the attacks hit
Eye Beam and Time Stop affect every other living thing - mobs of every kind and other players
(friendly fire is intentional, same as the rest of the set) - but never the wearer, or players in
creative/spectator.

## Controls
Both keys can be rebound under **Options > Controls**, in the "To Infinitite and Beyond" section.

## Visual Appearance
- Same pink-purple cosmic look as the rest of the Universal line.
- **Animated inventory icon** with a glowing visor band, same frame count and timing as the rest
  of the set (generated by `gen_universal_helmet_texture.py`).
- On the body it uses the set's shared `universal_armor` layer.

## Crafting
Five Universal Shards in the vanilla helmet shape:
```
S S S
S   S
```

## Design Notes
- Time Stop turns frozen mobs' AI off. So a mob can never be left frozen for good, they're
  unfrozen when the server shuts down, and a mob saved mid-freeze (e.g. the game crashed) is
  unfrozen as soon as it loads back in.
