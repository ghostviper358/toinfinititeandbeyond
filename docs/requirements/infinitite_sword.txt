INFINITITE SWORD MOD REQUIREMENTS
=====================================
Created for: Jasper
Date: October 14, 2025

OVERVIEW
--------
A powerful mystical sword made from a new material called "Infinitite". The sword has a black blade with purple veins that glow with mystical energy, and it's stronger than diamond swords with special magical effects.

NEW MATERIAL: INFINITITE
------------------------
Name: Infinitite
Recipe: 4 Diamonds + 5 Iron Ingots = 1 Infinitite Ingot
Crafting Location: Standard crafting table
Purpose: Used to create the Infinitite Sword

INFINITITE SWORD SPECIFICATIONS
------------------------------

CRAFTING RECIPE:
- Uses standard sword crafting pattern
- 2 Infinitite Ingots + 1 Stick = 1 Infinitite Sword
- Crafted at: Regular crafting table

APPEARANCE:
- Blade Color: Black
- Special Design: Purple veins running through the blade
- Glow Effect: Steady mystical purple glow (same color as veins)
- Overall Look: Dark and mystical

COMBAT STATS:
- Attack Damage: 11 (compared to Diamond sword's 7)
- Durability: 3124 (twice as much as Diamond sword's 1562)
- Attack Speed: Same as Diamond sword

SPECIAL ABILITIES:
- Blindness Effect: Enemies hit by the sword get blinded
- Poison Effect: Enemies hit by the sword get poisoned
- Effect Duration: To be determined during development
- Affects: All enemies and players hit by the sword

TECHNICAL NOTES:
- Infinitite material will need custom textures
- Sword will need custom model with glow effect
- Special effects will trigger on successful hit
- Glow should be visible in dark areas
- Purple color should match between veins and glow

FUTURE CONSIDERATIONS:
- Effect duration balancing
- Possible additional Infinitite tools/armor
- Sound effects for the sword
- Particle effects when swinging
