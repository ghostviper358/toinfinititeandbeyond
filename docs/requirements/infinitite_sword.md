# Infinitite Sword Requirements

## Overview
A powerful magical sword made from the mystical Infinitite metal. This legendary weapon combines the strength of iron with the magical properties of diamonds to create something truly extraordinary.

## Infinitite Material

### Crafting Recipe
- **Input**: 4 Diamonds + 5 Iron Ingots
- **Output**: 1 Infinitite Ingot
- **Crafting Method**: Standard crafting table recipe
- **Rarity**: Very rare due to expensive materials needed

### Properties
- Created by combining the magical essence of diamonds with the durability of iron
- Results in a mystical black metal with purple magical veins

## Infinitite Sword Stats

### Combat Properties
- **Attack Damage**: 11 (compared to Diamond Sword's 7, Netherite Sword's 8)
- **Durability**: Twice as long as a Diamond Sword
- **Special Effects**: Inflicts Blindness and Poison on hit

### Visual Appearance
- **Base Color**: Deep black blade
- **Accent Color**: Mystical purple veins running through the blade
- **Glow Effect**: Steady mystical purple glow (same color as the veins)
- **Overall Look**: Dark and mysterious with magical energy radiating from it

## Special Abilities

### Combat Effects
- **Blindness Effect**: Applied to enemies/players when hit (lasts 3 seconds)
- **Poison Effect**: Applied to enemies/players when hit (lasts 3 seconds)
- Both effects trigger on every successful hit

### Enhanced Durability
- Lasts twice as long as a Diamond Sword before breaking
- Makes it a valuable long-term weapon investment

## Implementation Notes
- Sword should be crafted using standard sword recipe with Infinitite Ingots
- Requires 2 Infinitite Ingots + 1 Stick = 1 Infinitite Sword
- Total material cost per sword: 8 Diamonds + 10 Iron Ingots + 1 Stick
