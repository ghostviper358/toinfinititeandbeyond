# Universal Boots Requirements

## Overview
The third piece of the **Universal armor set**, after the Universal Chestplate and Leggings (see
`docs/requirements/universal_chestplate.md` and `universal_leggings.md`). Same "absurdly
overpowered on purpose" scale, focused on where you can go: the sky, straight down, and across
water and lava.

## Defensive Stats
Same as the chestplate and leggings:
- **Armor: 999**, **Toughness: 6**, **40% knockback resistance**.
- **+40 max health (20 extra hearts)** while worn, stacking with the other pieces.
- **Durability: unbreakable**.

## Abilities

### Cosmic Flight
- Fly like in creative mode while worn (**double-tap jump** to take off), leaving a trail of
  stars and purple sparkles.
- Taking the boots off takes the flight away again - unless you're in creative/spectator, where
  you could already fly.

### Meteor Slam
- **Sneak in mid-air** (while not flying) to rocket straight down.
- Landing makes a huge blast: everything within **8 blocks** takes **300 damage** and is
  launched outward and up. The blast doesn't break blocks.
- **No fall damage, ever**, while the boots are worn.

### Liquid Walking
- Walk on top of **water and lava** as if it were solid ground.
- **Sneak** to sink in and swim normally.
- Standing on lava doesn't set you on fire (falling into it still would).

### Supernova Trail
- Walking leaves patches of burning starfire behind you, each lasting **3 seconds**.
- Anything that steps in it takes **20 damage** and is **set on fire for 5 seconds**.

## Who the attacks hit
Meteor Slam and Supernova Trail hit hostile mobs and other players (friendly fire is intentional,
same as the Universal Sword's spin), but never the wearer, or players in creative/spectator.

## Visual Appearance
- Same pink-purple cosmic look as the rest of the Universal line.
- **Animated inventory icon**, same frame count and timing as the chestplate and leggings
  (generated by `gen_universal_boots_texture.py`).
- On the body they use the set's shared `universal_armor` layer.

## Crafting
Four Universal Shards in the vanilla boots shape:
```
S   S
S   S
```

## Design Notes
- Meteor Slam starts on a single sneak press in mid-air, so a mid-air double-tap of sneak with
  the Universal Leggings on slams you down before the Warp Dash. Warp Dash on the ground is
  unaffected.
