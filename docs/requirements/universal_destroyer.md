# The Universal Destroyer

The final boss: the ancient terror from before the universe that the lore books warn about. The
Monstrosity guarded the door, the Living Void was its jailer, and the Void Seal is the last lock.

## Summoning

- The Living Void drops a **Void Seal** (a glowing block of black obsidian with a ring of purple
  runes, darkness seeping out of it).
- Place it and break it: the ritual begins. The ground shakes, purple lightning strikes, a storm
  rolls in, and after 6 seconds the Destroyer rises.
- Only one Destroyer within 128 blocks. If one is already there, the seal holds and pops back out.
- Explosions and pistons can't break or move the seal.

## Looks

- A horned titan of black stone plates veined with glowing purple, crowned with broken black
  spikes. Spiked shoulder armor, spikes down its back, a jutting jaw with a glowing mouth, a belt
  with a glowing buckle, a chest plate with a glowing core and a great rune. Knees and elbows bend.
- **About 46 blocks tall.** Minecraft caps a mob's SCALE at 16x, so it's 16x and the renderer draws
  it 1.25x bigger still (20x in all).
- **Hovers 5 blocks above the ground**, with dark energy streaming down beneath it.
- **Big hitbox**: 36 blocks wide and 56 tall, covering its whole body (head included), from the
  ground up past its crown, so you can still hit it with a sword from the ground.
- Wields a colossal black-metal sword with a winged guard, runes carved down the blade and a
  Universal Core in the guard.
- Textures painted at 4x detail.
- Its own boss bar: its horned, crowned head, a black rune frame, a purple bar (red in phase 3).

## Fight

100,000 health (50,000 hearts). Glides through terrain toward its target. Three phases, each
starting with a roar that throws everyone back (it can't be hurt while roaring):

1. **Phase 1**: Sword Sweep (with a backhand), Overhead Smash (sends out **Shockwave Rings**: jump
   over them!), Rune Circles, Star Fall.
2. **Phase 2 "Unsealed"** (below 2/3 health): a thunderstorm rolls in. Adds Sword Wave, Gravity
   Crush, Black Hole, **Void Chains** (grabs the nearest 2 players, lifts them high and slams them
   down) and **Shadow Swords** (giant ghostly swords hang in the sky over you, then stab down).
3. **Phase 3 "Annihilation"** (below 1/3): night falls, stars rain down constantly, every vein
   blazes and its eyes burn red. Adds Cosmic Annihilation (a beam from the core in its sword,
   down to the ground, sweeping a full circle as it turns: jump it!) and **Supernova** (4 seconds
   of charging, then a huge explosion. The closer you are, the more it hurts, so run!).

The Universal Sword can't instantly kill or fling it (its lightning just hits hard), and a
Universal God can't delete it.

## The finale: climb the sword

- No hit can take it below 10,000 health until this happens, so the finale can't be skipped.
- Below 10,000 health **its whole body starts to glow**, pulsing.
- It holds up its sword, **snaps it in two**, and hurls the blade into the ground in front of it
  as a ramp of solid black stairs, sparkling with light. Then it kneels.
- While it kneels **its body is solid: you can walk on it**. Run up the sword, step onto its
  shoulders and walk across to its face (a purple ring marks it).
- **Strike its face within 3 minutes.** Swinging at it up there counts, even if your crosshair is
  off its hitbox. Hits anywhere else bounce off.
- If nobody makes it in time, **every player nearby takes 1,000,000 damage**: only a Universal God
  survives. Then the countdown starts again.
- A face strike deals 4,000 damage, the ramp crumbles (everyone gets slow falling, so the drop is
  safe), and it fights on with its broken sword. It can no longer use the sword-swinging attacks.

## Ending and drops

When it dies: "THE UNIVERSE IS SAVED", the credits in chat, the storm clears to a starry night,
and fireworks go off around every player.

Drops:
- 32-64 Universal Shards
- **The Universal Core** from its sword (eat it to become a Universal God)
- **The Destroyer's Crown**: a trophy helmet. 30 armor, 10 toughness, unbreakable, enchanted
  shimmer and a purple glow around you. No special powers.
- The final lore book, "The Stars Return"
- 5000 XP
