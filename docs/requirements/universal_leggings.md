# Universal Leggings Requirements

## Overview
The second piece of the **Universal armor set**, after the Universal Chestplate (see
`docs/requirements/universal_chestplate.md`). Same "absurdly overpowered on purpose" scale as the
rest of the Universal gear, focused on movement: speed, teleporting, dodging, and extra jumps.

## Defensive Stats
- **Armor: 999** - the set's signature number, same as the chestplate.
- **Armor Toughness: 6** and **40% knockback resistance** - same as the chestplate.
- **+40 max health (20 extra hearts)** while worn. Stacks on top of the chestplate's own +40,
  so wearing both gives 40 extra hearts.
- **Durability: unbreakable** (vanilla `minecraft:unbreakable` data component).

## Abilities

### Star Speed
- **+50% running speed** while worn (on top of the Speed effect below).
- A trail of stars and purple sparkles left behind at your feet whenever you're moving.

### Warp Dash
- **Double-tap Shift (sneak)** to teleport about **12 blocks** in the direction you're looking.
- Stops short of walls instead of going through them.
- Everything you dash through takes **200 damage**.
- **2 second cooldown.**

### Cosmic Phase
- **30% chance** to phase out and completely dodge any attack from a mob or player (melee or
  ranged). You flicker invisible for a moment, with a burst of purple particles.

### Star Jumps
- **5 extra jumps in mid-air.** They refill when you land. No fall damage from a fall you broke
  with a Star Jump.

### Every Good Effect
All beneficial potion effects, permanently while worn: Speed II, Haste III, Strength III,
Jump Boost II, Regeneration II, Resistance II, Fire Resistance, Water Breathing, Night Vision,
Saturation, Luck, Conduit Power, Dolphin's Grace, Hero of the Village, and Absorption II.

Left out on purpose:
- **Invisibility** - would hide the wearer and all the set's glowing effects.
- **Slow Falling** - would cancel out the Star Jumps.
- **Health Boost** - the extra hearts already come from the leggings' +40 max health.
- Instant effects (Instant Health) - not lasting effects.

## Visual Appearance
- Same pink-purple cosmic look as the rest of the Universal line.
- **Animated inventory icon** - a swirl flowing down the legs, same frame count and timing as
  the chestplate's icon (generated by `gen_universal_leggings_texture.py`).
- On the body they use the set's shared `universal_armor` leggings layer.

## Crafting

