# To Infinitite And Beyond

Mine the void, forge Infinitite, and climb from overpowered to **completely ridiculous**. Three giant bosses stand between you and the Universal Core, the item that turns you into a god.

This mod is not balanced, and it isn't trying to be. Every tier of gear is stronger than the last, until you're flying through walls and deleting mobs with a keypress.

---

## The gear

### Void gear: deep, dark and nasty
Mine **Void Ore** at the very bottom of the world (Y -64 to -50) for **Void Ingots**.
- **Void Sword**: Wither, Darkness and Soul Drain on every hit, and it pulls enemies toward you. Every 5th hit on the same target permanently steals one of its hearts.
- **Void Armor**: each piece has its own dark powers, including a Wither aura, a void shield, teleport-dashing when you sprint, warden-style sonic blasts, shockwave landings and huge permanent buffs.

### Infinitite gear: glowing purple power
- **Infinitite Sword**: blinds and poisons whatever it hits.
- **Infinitite Bow**: locks on to a target, and its arrows fly dead straight and home in.
- **Infinitite Armor**: tougher than Netherite, with Regeneration, Resistance, Speed and Strength, damage reflection and 5 extra hearts. The helmet gives you night vision and ore X-ray, and makes mobs glow through walls. The leggings make you invisible when you sneak and let you ground-slam from mid-air.

### Black Hole Arm Cannon
A cannon worn over your arm. Sneak + right-click switches between four attacks:
1. **Homing Blocks**: a machine-gun stream of exploding blocks that chase your target.
2. **Infinitite Spikes**: a ring of giant purple spikes bursts out of the ground.
3. **Black Hole**: drags everything in and eats nearby blocks to refill your ammo. *Unlocked by beating the Living Void.*
4. **Void Lightning**: black lightning that chains from target to target. *Powered up for 10 minutes each time you beat a boss.*

### Universal gear: the top of the ladder
Crafted from **Universal Shards**, which only the bosses drop.
- **Universal Sword**: 999 damage and unbreakable. Hold attack to charge a shockwave of five rings that wipes out everything around you.
- **Universal Armor**: 999 armor per piece and over 100 extra hearts for the full set. You get creative-style flight, warp dashes, extra mid-air jumps, walking on lava, a **Cosmic Eye Beam** (R) and **Time Stop** (G), which freezes everything around you for 5 seconds.
- **Cosmic Apple**: a minute of total invincibility, plus 30 extra max health, permanently.

## The bosses

### 1. The Infinitite Monstrosity
A towering golem of dark rock split by glowing purple cracks, with 500 hearts. It throws exploding rocks, slams the ground and sends cracks racing toward you. At half health it powers up and adds a sweeping void beam, a black hole and void lightning. **To summon it**, throw **8 Void Ingots and a Nether Star** on the ground together. The earth shakes, and it climbs out.

### 2. The Living Void
A giant floating eye of darkness. It opens rifts that spit out Endermen and Vexes, flips gravity, blinds you and fires homing void orbs. **To summon it**, throw a **Monstrosity Heart** on the ground with **10 Infinitite Ingots and 1 Universal Shard**.

### 3. The Universal Destroyer
The final boss: a 46-block-tall horned titan with **100,000 health** and three phases of attacks, including star falls, shadow swords, gravity crushes and a supernova. Win the fight and you get an ending you won't forget. **To summon it**, place the **Void Seal** that the Living Void drops, then break it.

Each boss drops a lore book that tells part of the story. The Destroyer drops the **Destroyer's Crown** and the **Universal Core**...

## Become a Universal God
Eat the **Universal Core** and you become a god, permanently. You're truly invincible, you never get hungry, and you can fly straight through blocks. Your god powers have their own keys:

| Key | Power |
|---|---|
| H | Summon a meteor |
| J | Lightning storm |
| K | Delete a mob |
| N | Switch day/night (sneak + N: change the weather) |

The only thing that can hurt a god is another god.

> ⚠️ **Server owners:** godhood is permanent and makes a player invincible. Think about that before adding this mod to a shared survival server.

## Getting started
1. Dig down to the bottom of the world and mine **Void Ore** to make Void gear.
2. Beat the Wither for a **Nether Star**, then throw it down with 8 Void Ingots to summon the **Infinitite Monstrosity**.
3. The Monstrosity drops **Infinitite Ingots**, and each boss drops what you need to summon the next one.
- Install **JEI** (Just Enough Items) to look up every recipe in the game. The armor and swords use the normal vanilla shapes.
- Every keybind can be changed under **Options → Controls → Key Binds → To Infinitite and Beyond**.
